Our Services

From residential windows to large commercial complexes — HITECH delivers excellence at every scale.

Residential Aluminium Works

Premium aluminium sliding windows, casement windows, aluminium doors, mosquito mesh frames, staircase railings, and balcony glass railing systems for homes and apartments.

  • Sliding & casement windows
  • Aluminium doors & frames
  • Balcony railings & grills
  • Mosquito mesh systems

Commercial Building Facades

Complete structural glazing, spider glass systems, curtain walling, and aluminium cladding solutions for offices, hospitals, hotels, malls, and industrial buildings.

  • Structural glazing systems
  • Curtain wall facade
  • ACP cladding
  • Spider glass facade

Textile Shop Racks & Displays

Bespoke aluminium and steel rack systems, display towers, wall-mounted rail systems, and full interior fitouts for textile showrooms, boutiques, and garment stores.

  • Wall-mounted display rails
  • Floor-standing rack towers
  • Fitting room structures
  • Cash counter fabrication

Hypermarket & Supermarket Setups

Complete shelving, gondola rack systems, refrigeration unit framing, checkout counters, and full interior setup for hypermarkets, supermarkets, and large retail stores.

  • Gondola shelving systems
  • Checkout counter fabrication
  • Cold storage framing
  • Storage room racking

Custom Workshop Fabrication

Our state-of-the-art Kollam workshop handles all custom aluminium fabrication — precision CNC cutting, TIG welding, powder coating, and anodizing for any project requirement.

  • CNC precision cutting
  • TIG / MIG welding
  • Powder coating (all colors)
  • Anodizing services

Partitions & Glazing Systems

Modern aluminium-framed glass partition systems for offices, hospitals, showrooms, and corporate spaces. Full-height, demountable, and frameless options available.

  • Full-height glass partitions
  • Demountable partitions
  • Frameless glass systems
  • Frosted / tinted options
